An entry for the [Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a) bounty on make.fun: one original sentence, no more than 200 characters, pitching a startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke, yet specific enough that a reader can see the customers and the money.

## The line

> We sell life insurance for houseplants: owners pay a monthly premium, our sitters water them while they are away, and if one still dies, we pay out — cheaper than the guilt.

Life insurance for a fern sounds absurd until the second look: plant owners already spend real money on rare monsteras and fiddle-leaf figs, plant sitters are a real gig, and the math works — most insured plants survive because someone is paid to water them, so premiums outrun payouts and the only thing being sold, really, is peace of mind.

python3 main.py
